Grant the user Content Catalog / Reader (or Editor) access in addition to their
normal Sales permissions. Open a saved quotation or order and click
**Content Catalog**. Browse subject cards filtered by the products on the order;
clear or change the product filter to consult other products or company content.

Open a subject and use **Information**, **Materials** and **FAQ** to read texts,
inspect images or PDFs, copy an answer and download files. Close the popup to
return to the quotation. The button does not alter order lines, attach files to
emails or send material to the customer. Content remains private to authorized
users of the relevant company.
