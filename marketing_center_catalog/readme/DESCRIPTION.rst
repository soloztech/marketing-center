A shared, editable library of company and product information for Odoo 16.
Marketing and engineering maintain subjects containing formatted text, FAQs,
files and external links. Sales and support can consult the same current source.

This standalone application does not require the Marketing Center analytics
suite. Optional addons connect the library to Sales and Contact Center.

* Save content directly, edit it in place and archive it when no longer useful.
* Keep structured information and frequently asked questions together in each subject.
* Link subjects to product templates, individual variants or both.
* Restrict individual items to specific products when necessary.
* Distinguish internal information from content that can be shared with customers.
* Keep files private and isolate each company's library.

There is no editorial approval, publishing workflow, immutable revision archive,
public portal or AI service in this initial version.
