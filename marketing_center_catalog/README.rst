A shared, editable library of company and product information for Odoo 16.
Marketing and engineering maintain subjects containing formatted text, FAQs,
files and external links. Sales and support can consult the same current source.

This standalone application does not require the Marketing Center analytics
suite. Optional addons connect the library to Sales and Contact Center.

* Save content directly, edit it in place and archive it when no longer useful.
* Keep structured information and frequently asked questions together in each subject.
* Link subjects to product templates, individual variants or both.
* Restrict individual items to specific products when necessary.
* Distinguish internal information from content that can be shared with customers.
* Keep files private and isolate each company's library.

There is no editorial approval, publishing workflow, immutable revision archive,
public portal or AI service in this initial version.

Configuration
=============

In Settings / Users, grant Content Catalog / Editor to the people maintaining
the library and Content Catalog / Reader to the people consulting it.
Administrators inherit editor access. Catalog access is separate from sales,
support and advertising analytics roles.

Each subject belongs to one company. Linked products must either belong to that
company or be shared products. Company record rules apply to both subjects and
content, including downloads through Odoo's authenticated binary route.

Usage
=====

Open Content Catalog / Subjects and create a company or solution subject.
Use the Information tab for Structured Information and, directly below it,
Frequently Asked Questions. Use Materials for one file per item or external
HTTP(S) links. Saving makes content available immediately. Mark items as
shareable only when they may be sent to customers.

Structured Information, Frequently Asked Questions and Materials each offer
independent List and Kanban views. Information and questions start in List;
Materials starts in Kanban. Use the view buttons to change the layout while
working on the same subject.

The Frequently Asked Questions list shows only Question and Answer. Add or edit
them directly in the list; answers support formatted text. New FAQs use the
question as their title when no title was supplied. Existing custom titles are
preserved. Switch to
Kanban and open a card to edit additional settings, such as sharing permissions.

Link a product template to include all its variants. Linking one variant only
includes that variant. Leave subject product links empty for information that
applies to all products, such as company information. Item product restrictions
intersect with the subject links; they never broaden the subject's scope.
When no product is selected, all items remain discoverable and show applicability.

The Content button on templates and variants opens a company-scoped library with
removable product filters. Start with the subject cards, then browse Information,
Materials and FAQ. Readers can open previews, download files and copy text.
Archive an item or subject to remove it from library searches. Archived records
remain accessible to authorized users through the regular management screens.

Optional integrations:

* ``marketing_center_catalog_sale`` adds the library to quotations and orders.
* ``marketing_center_catalog_contact_center`` adds content to a conversation draft,
  using independent attachment copies and the normal message composer.

Editing or archiving the library does not rewrite previously prepared drafts or
sent messages. Native record identifiers and modification dates support later
integrations, without promising immutable historical sources.

Subjects and the content library open as native kanban cards. Uploaded photos
render directly; PDF cards show the first page when visible, and opening a PDF
shows the document viewer. Supported uploaded videos play on demand. Other files
remain downloadable. External links open in another tab.

Photos, PDF documents and videos remain ordinary File items: their preview format
is detected automatically. The optional material category describes the purpose
(photo, catalog, datasheet, manual and so on). Materials only offers File or Link;
Structured Information creates text and Frequently Asked Questions creates
questions and answers, both within the Information tab.
