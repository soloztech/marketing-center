Both repositories must be on the Odoo addons path: ``marketing-center`` for the
catalog and ``contact-center`` for ``contact_center_ui``. Install this optional
bridge and add the **Catalog Reader** group to agents who need catalog access.
They also need access to their Contact Center accounts and conversations.

Editors maintain company-specific subjects and content in the catalog app. Mark
items **Can Share with Customer** to make them selectable in the conversation
popup. Internal items remain available for consultation only.
